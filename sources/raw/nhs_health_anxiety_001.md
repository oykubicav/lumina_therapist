## Key content captured (paraphrased structure, full text retained here as research reference only)

Signs the page lists:
- Constant worry about health
- Frequent body checking for signs of illness (lumps, tingling, pain)
- Repeatedly asking others for reassurance about not being ill
- Worrying that a doctor or medical tests may have missed something
- Obsessively reviewing health information online or in media
- Avoiding anything related to serious illness (e.g., medical TV programmes)
- Acting as if one were ill (e.g., avoiding physical activities)

Important framing: anxiety itself can cause symptoms (headaches, racing heart) that are mistaken for illness.

Self-help suggestions listed:
- Keep a diary of checking, reassurance-seeking, and information-searching frequency; reduce gradually
- Two-column thought challenge (worry vs. balanced thought)
- Distraction (walk, call a friend) when urge to check arises
- Gradual return to avoided normal activities
- Relaxation / breathing exercises

When to see a GP: worries prevent normal life, self-help not working.
Treatment options: CBT, anxiety medication, self-referral to NHS talking therapies.
