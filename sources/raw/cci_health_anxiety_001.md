## Publisher framing (direct quote of consumer landing text)

"To worry about your health is a normal human experience. Health anxiety refers to the excessive concern that there may be a threat to your health which triggers your anxiety (fight/flight) response. Health anxiety is problematic when it is excessive, out of proportion to the realistic chances of having a serious problem, persists despite negative tests and reassurance from health professionals, leads to excessive unhelpful behaviours such as body-checking and medical test-seeking, and causes you significant distress or impacts on your functioning."

Key clinical framing captured:
- Health anxiety exists on a continuum
- Real medical symptoms + health anxiety can coexist
- Framing shift: "the issue is not whether physical symptoms are real, but whether you are responding to and coping with your symptoms in a helpful or unhelpful way"

3. **What Keeps Health Anxiety Going?** — maintenance cycle

5. **Re-evaluating Unhelpful Health Related Thinking** — cognitive restructuring
6. **Reducing Checking and Reassurance Seeking** — behavioural experiment
7. **Challenging Avoidance and Safety Behaviours** — exposure hierarchy
8. **Adjusting Health Rules and Assumptions** — schema-level work
9. **Healthy Living and Self-Management Planning** — relapse prevention
