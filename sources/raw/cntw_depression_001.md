## Brief content visible on hub:
- "No one cause for depression has been found, but it is likely that early experiences, difficult circumstances, individual differences, body chemistry, reduced activity and gloomy thoughts can all play an important role in depression."
- "Most people are treated for depression by their family doctor. The doctor may suggest self help (like this), a talking therapy, antidepressant tablets or a mixture of all three."
- "One way of dealing with rumination is to try and notice what's going on when this sort of thinking starts, and try to do something different, such as: getting active, practicing relaxation, reading or doing a puzzle."
