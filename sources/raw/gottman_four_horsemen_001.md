## Central concept — Four Horsemen predict relationship dissolution

The Gottman Method identifies four communication patterns that empirically predict divorce/dissolution:

1. **Criticism** — ad hominem attack on partner's character (not a specific complaint about behavior)
2. **Contempt** — communicating from a position of moral superiority; mocking, sarcasm, name-calling, eye-rolling. **Single greatest predictor of divorce.**
3. **Defensiveness** — response to criticism; playing innocent victim, reversing blame
4. **Stonewalling** — withdrawal from interaction; physiological flooding; shutdown

## Verbatim examples captured

**Criticism vs Complaint (distinction):**
- Complaint (healthy): "I was scared when you were running late and didn't call me. I thought we had agreed that we would do that for each other."
- Criticism (harmful): "You never think about how your behavior is affecting other people. I don't believe you are that forgetful, you're just selfish. You never think of others! You never think of me!"

**Contempt (example given):**
"You're 'tired?' Cry me a river. I've been with the kids all day, running around like mad to keep this house going and all you do when you come home from work is flop down on that sofa like a child and play those idiotic video games. I don't have time to deal with another kid. Could you be any more pathetic?"

**Defensiveness (example given):**
- Q: "Did you call Betty and Ralph to let them know that we're not coming tonight as you promised this morning?"
- Defensive: "I was just too darn busy today. As a matter of fact, you know just how busy my schedule was. Why didn't you just do it?"
- Non-defensive (antidote): "Oops, I forgot. I should have asked you this morning to do it because I knew my day would be packed. That's my fault. Let me call them right now."

**Stonewalling antidote (self-regulation):**
"Alright, I'm feeling too angry to keep talking about this. Can we please take a break and come back to it in a bit? It'll be easier to work through this after I've calmed down."

Then take 20 minutes to do something soothing alone before returning.

## Research basis

Gottman Institute cites 40+ years of longitudinal research on couples. The Four Horsemen framework has been validated in the "Love Lab" (Seattle) via observational coding of couple interactions predicting outcomes. Contempt correlated with weakened immune systems (Kiecolt-Glaser research collaborations).
