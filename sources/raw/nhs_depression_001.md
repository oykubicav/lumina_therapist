## Use in our product

NHS frames depression as: persistent (weeks/months), pervasive (affects daily life), and treatable. The stepped-care framing (self-help → talking therapy → medication → specialist team) is the canonical NHS / NICE pathway and aligns with our chatbot's "this is the kind of thing where you should see your GP / psychiatrist / clinical psychologist" framing. The separation of "low mood" from "depression" is useful for our triage: not every user with a bad week needs the full depression module.

UK-specific elements (NHS GP referral, NHS talking therapies self-referral, Samaritans, Mind, Anxiety UK) must NOT be transferred to TR product.
